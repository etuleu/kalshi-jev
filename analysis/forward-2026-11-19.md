## Interpretation

1. **No demonstrated model edge.** Eight cases are far too few, and no model beats the simple price/volatility proxy on Brier score. Some models beat this sample's market Brier, but the differences are not established improvements.
2. **The larger LLMs largely track the simple proxy.** Mean absolute differences in UP probability were about 1.0 percentage point for DeepSeek, 1.5 for GLM, and 1.6 for Kimi. DeepSeek made exactly the proxy's two trades and earned the same pre-inference paper P&L. This sample shows no incremental trading value from that inference.
3. **Drex was more bullish than the simple proxy in this sample.** Drex Max assigned approximately 91% UP to the 06:35 observation and 89% UP to 07:05; both markets resolved DOWN. Its four trades lost $0.80, gained $0.72, lost $0.74, and gained $0.25. Only two winning trades do not imply profitability.
4. **Positive totals are fragile.** DeepSeek/proxy's +$0.27 comes from one +$0.67 winner and one -$0.40 loser. That is not a repeatable-edge estimate.
5. **Always-50% is a contrarian strategy here, not random betting.** The entry rule buys a sufficiently cheap YES or NO whenever a 50% forecast indicates enough edge. Its +$0.59 is a tiny-sample result, not proof of arbitrage or a recommendation.
6. **Costs matter at this paper size.** Kimi incurred about $0.219 of inference cost over eight windows while producing the same paper trades as GLM. Costs, real execution, and fee uncertainty could erase small gross advantages.
